ABSTRACT

For high-speed, motion-blurred, and low-light scenes, this work introduces an event-camera keypoint detection and tracking pipeline. Event density, polarity-adaptive matching, EKF, and EPnP enable stable 6-DoF pose estimation for dynamic objects.
